The house

A village house at the entrance to Mézin

Le Relais de Gascogne is run by Valérie and Etienne Soufflet. White front, blue shutters and geraniums: you will find it on avenue Max Grosselle, as you arrive from Nérac on the RD 656.

In Armagnaccountry

In the land of Armagnac, foie gras and prunes

At the southern tip of Lot-et-Garonne, Mézin is 10 km from the Gers and the Landes. Around the Relais: abbeys, castles and museums, including the Armagnac museum in Condom.

Cork museum in Mézin · Henri IV castle in Nérac · Flaran Abbey · Séviac Gallo-Roman villa · Armagnac museum in Condom · The Way of St James
